Privacy policy

Your privacy, plainly stated

How Abundant Corner Counselling collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Who we are

Abundant Corner Counselling is the virtual therapy practice of Yeukai Tsikira, Registered Psychotherapist (Qualifying), CRPO 18718, and Canadian Certified Counsellor, CCPA 11246478. Contact: info@abundantcornercounselling.com or 780-306-1676.

Information we collect

Contact form: if you write through the form on this site, we receive your name, email address and message, sent to Yeukai by email so she can reply. Booking: when you book, JaneApp — the practice platform used for booking and billing — collects the name, contact details and appointment information you enter there; that information is governed by Jane’s privacy policy. Website use: this site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use it — which pages are visited, roughly where visitors are, and what device they use. Google Analytics sets cookies and may process this information on servers outside Canada. It does not collect your name, email, or anything you type into the contact form, and no advertising trackers are used. The only other thing saved in your browser is your language choice (English or French). Google Fonts are loaded from Google’s servers to display text.

How we use it

To reply to you, to book and provide your sessions, and to send receipts. We do not sell your information and we do not share it for marketing.

Clinical records and confidentiality

Session notes are kept as long as required by her professional regulator and the law. What you share in therapy is confidential, with the standard limits every therapist explains at the start: risk of serious harm to yourself or someone else, child protection concerns, and a court order.

Your rights

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask for a correction, or ask questions about how it is handled — write to info@abundantcornercounselling.com.
