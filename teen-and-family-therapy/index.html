Teen & family therapy · Virtual across Canada

Support for teens 12+ and families

For teens dealing with anxiety, mood changes, school pressure, and hard seasons, and for families working through conflict, change and loss, together.

Teens 12 and up can meet one-to-one online, at a pace that fits them. Family sessions bring the people who matter into the room, to work on communicating and understanding each other.

Family sessions are $180 for 75 minutes. Sessions are by secure video, from anywhere in Canada, in English. Receipts are provided for insurance benefits.

Questions, answered

Straight answers before you book. Anything else — ask in your free consultation.

How old does my teen need to be for therapy?

Teen therapy is for ages 12 and older, by secure video, in English and French. Sessions are 50 minutes and cost $120, the same as individual therapy for adults.

What is family therapy?

Family therapy is for families who want to work through things together. Sessions are 75 minutes and cost $180, by secure video.

Do we need a referral for teen or family therapy?

No. You do not need a referral. Start with a free 15-minute consultation to talk through who will attend and how sessions work.

Is what my teen shares confidential?

Confidentiality is talked through together with the teen and their parent or guardian, so everyone understands what is shared and what stays private. Yeukai will explain the limits clearly in the first session.

Are teen and family sessions virtual?

All sessions are virtual, by secure video, across Canada, in English and French. Teens join from a private place, using a device with a camera and microphone.

Can we get a receipt for insurance?

Yes. Receipts are provided after every paid session. Check your own plan for coverage of Registered Psychotherapist (Qualifying) or Canadian Certified Counsellor services. There is no direct billing.

What happens if we need to cancel?

Please give at least 12 hours' notice. Your first late cancellation or missed appointment is not charged; after that, the full session fee applies.

Does the $50 promotion apply to family therapy?

The limited $50-per-session promotion applies to ongoing individual therapy only. It does not apply to family therapy or EMDR.
