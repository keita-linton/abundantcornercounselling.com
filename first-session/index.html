Your first session · Free 15-minute consultation first

Your first session, explained simply

Starting therapy can feel like a big step, especially when you do not know what will happen. This page walks you through the free consultation, the first paid session, and how virtual sessions work — so you can arrive knowing what to expect.

Step 1: the free 15-minute consultation

A short, no-pressure conversation — not a therapy session.

Everything starts with a free 15-minute consultation, booked through JaneApp, which opens in the same tab. Same-day consultation bookings are available, and Yeukai replies within one business day. You do not need a referral, and you do not need to prepare anything.

In those 15 minutes, you meet Yeukai by secure video, share a little about what brings you here, and ask whatever is on your mind — about fees, about how sessions work, about whether therapy feels right for you.

If you are booking for a teen aged 12 or older, or for family therapy, the consultation is a good time to talk through who will attend and how those sessions work.

Step 2: your first paid session

Unhurried, warm, and led by you.

Your first paid session is 50 minutes for individual therapy, 75 minutes for family therapy, or 90 minutes for EMDR. It is by secure video, from a private place that feels comfortable to you.

Yeukai will start by welcoming you, checking how you are doing, and talking through what you hope therapy can help with. You might talk about what has been hard lately, how long it has been going on, and what you would like to feel different.

Toward the end, you and Yeukai talk about next steps: whether to meet again, how often, and what you would like to focus on. You may leave with a small, realistic idea to try between sessions — never a long list of tasks.

How virtual sessions work

Secure video, from wherever you feel most comfortable in Canada.

All sessions are virtual, by secure video. After you book through JaneApp, you receive a link to join your session at the booked time. You will need a device with a camera and microphone and a steady internet connection, and a private, quiet place where you will not be interrupted.

If the connection drops or the sound cuts out, do not worry — Yeukai will reconnect with you, and the time is handled fairly. If you are new to video calls, the free consultation is a good chance to try it out and to ask any technical questions before your first paid session.

Root-cause healing shapes the work from the start: we work on where the pain started, not just how to cope with it. That means therapy here looks beyond quick fixes, toward understanding the patterns and experiences underneath what you are feeling — always at a pace that feels safe for you.

Confidentiality

What you share stays between you and Yeukai, with a few legal limits.

What you share in session is confidential. Yeukai will not share what you tell her without your permission, except in the limited situations the law requires — for example, if there is a serious risk of harm to you or someone else, or if a court orders disclosure. She is a Registered Psychotherapist (Qualifying), CRPO registration 18718, and a Canadian Certified Counsellor (CCC), CCPA member 11246478.

For teens aged 12 and older, confidentiality is talked through together with the teen and their parent or guardian, so everyone understands what is shared and what stays private. If you have questions about confidentiality, ask in your free consultation — it is a good question, and it is welcome.

What should I bring to my first session?

Nothing. You do not need to bring notes, forms, or anything else. A private place and a device with a camera and microphone are all you need. If writing down a few thoughts beforehand helps you, you are welcome to do that.

What is the difference between the free consultation and a first session?

The free consultation is a 15-minute conversation to meet Yeukai, share a little about what brings you here, and see whether working together feels right. A first paid session is a full session — 50 minutes for individual therapy — where the therapeutic work begins.

Do I need a referral?

No. You do not need a referral from a doctor or anyone else to book a consultation or a session. Some benefits plans ask for a referral before they pay a claim, so check your own plan if you plan to claim.

Is what I share confidential?

Yes, with a few legal limits — for example, if there is a serious risk of harm to you or someone else, or if a court orders disclosure. Yeukai will explain those limits clearly in your first session.

What if I am nervous about video sessions?

That is very common. The free consultation is a good, low-pressure way to try secure video, ask technical questions, and see how it feels. You can join from a private, comfortable place, and headphones can help with privacy.
