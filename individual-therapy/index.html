Individual therapy · Virtual across Canada

One-to-one therapy for adults

For adults carrying anxiety, depression, grief, or the weight of always being the strong one. We go to the root cause, at your pace.

Survival mode can look like feeling on edge, numb, overwhelmed, or stuck in the same patterns, while still showing up for work, family and daily life. Individual therapy is a place to slow down and look at what is underneath, not only the symptoms on the surface.

Sessions are 50 minutes, by secure video, from anywhere in Canada. Individual sessions are $120 for 50 minutes, with receipts for your insurance benefits.

Who this is for

Adults who feel stuck in survival mode, getting through the day but never really settling, and who want to understand why and work toward healing where it started.

Everyone is welcome in this practice.

How sessions work

Meet online from a space that feels safe. We build a plan around your story, using talk therapy, EMDR where it fits, and other approaches at your pace.

You receive a receipt after each session to submit to your insurance benefits. Direct billing is not offered yet.

Questions, answered

Straight answers before you book. Anything else — ask in your free consultation.

What is individual therapy?

Individual therapy is for adults, one to one, by secure video. Sessions are 50 minutes and cost $120. A limited $50-per-session promotion is available for ongoing individual therapy only, while spots last.

What can individual therapy help with?

Individual therapy can help with anxiety, depression, grief, trauma and PTSD, life transitions, stress, burnout, and self-esteem. If you are unsure whether your concern fits, ask in your free 15-minute consultation.

Do I need a referral or a diagnosis?

No. You do not need a referral or a diagnosis. If something is affecting your daily life, your relationships, or how you feel about yourself, that is enough reason to book a free consultation.

Are sessions in person or virtual?

All sessions are virtual, by secure video, across Canada, in English and French. You join from a private, comfortable place, and you do not need to travel or sit in a waiting room.

How long will I need therapy?

It depends on your story and your goals. Some people notice a shift in a few sessions; others work together for longer. We review how things are going together, and you decide the pace.

Can I get a receipt for insurance?

Yes. Receipts are provided after every paid session. Check your own plan for coverage of Registered Psychotherapist (Qualifying) or Canadian Certified Counsellor services. There is no direct billing.

What happens if I need to cancel?

Please give at least 12 hours' notice. Your first late cancellation or missed appointment is not charged; after that, the full session fee applies.

Can I book a free consultation first?

Yes. Every first step starts with a free 15-minute consultation, booked through JaneApp. Same-day consultation bookings are available, and Yeukai replies within one business day.
