Anxiety therapy that goes to the root

If anxiety is running your days — the racing thoughts, the tight chest, the rehearsing, the avoiding — you do not have to keep managing it alone. Together we look at where it started, not just how to cope with it.

You might be here because…

Anxiety can look different from the outside than it feels on the inside.

You might be here because your mind rarely switches off. You replay conversations, worry about what could go wrong, and feel on edge even when nothing is happening. Sleep can be hard to come by, your body feels tense, and small decisions start to feel heavy. Maybe you avoid certain places, calls, or conversations — not because you do not care, but because the worry beforehand feels exhausting.

Or anxiety might show up at work, at school, or in relationships. You prepare and prepare, yet still feel unready. You say yes when you want to say no, or you hold back from saying what you really think. You might look calm and capable to everyone else while carrying a constant hum of dread inside. If any of that sounds familiar, you are in the right place, and you are not broken. Anxiety often makes sense once you understand what your mind and body learned to protect you from.

Root-cause healing for anxiety

We work on where the pain started, not just how to cope with it.

Coping tools have their place, and you will learn practical ways to steady yourself when anxiety spikes. But if we only work on the surface, the same worry tends to return in a new shape. Root-cause healing means we gently trace anxiety back to its roots — the experiences, messages, and moments that taught your nervous system to stay on alert.

That might include early experiences where you had to be careful, responsible, or perfect to feel safe. It might include a loss, a frightening event, or a season when life felt unpredictable. It might include beliefs you picked up along the way, like needing to be in control, or that rest has to be earned. In sessions, we notice those patterns together, in your words and in your body, and we work through them at a pace that feels safe. The goal is not to erase every worry. It is to help anxiety loosen its grip so you can live, choose, and rest more freely.

What therapy for anxiety looks like

Warm, plain, and practical — shaped around you, not a script.

Sessions are 50 minutes, by secure video, from wherever you feel most comfortable in Canada. We talk, we slow down, and we pay attention to what is happening for you in the moment. Yeukai draws on approaches she is trained in, including Trauma-Focused CBT, Internal Family Systems, Somatic Therapy, and Acceptance and Commitment Therapy, choosing what fits your story rather than forcing your story into one method.

Some sessions are mostly conversation. Others include grounding, breathing, or body-based work to help your nervous system settle. You might notice the parts of you that worry, criticise, or push you to keep going, and learn to meet them with more understanding. Between sessions, you may try small, realistic practices — not homework that adds pressure, but simple ways to notice and respond to anxiety differently. There is no right way to do therapy, and you will never be pushed faster than you are ready to go.

Do I need a diagnosis to start anxiety therapy?

No. You do not need a diagnosis or a referral. If anxiety is affecting your sleep, work, relationships, or daily life, that is enough reason to book a free consultation and talk it through.

How long does anxiety therapy take?

It depends on your story and your goals. Some people notice a shift in a few sessions; others work together for longer, especially when anxiety is tied to earlier experiences. We review how things are going together, and you decide the pace.

Will I have to talk about my childhood?

Only as much as feels useful and safe. Root-cause healing looks at where patterns began, which can include earlier life, but you choose what to share and when. We never push you into memories before you are ready.

What if I feel anxious about starting therapy?

That is very common, and it is welcome here. The free 15-minute consultation is a low-pressure way to meet Yeukai, ask questions, and see how a session feels before you commit to anything.

Is virtual therapy effective for anxiety?

Virtual therapy by secure video works well for many people with anxiety. You meet from a private, comfortable place, with no travel or waiting room. If virtual sessions do not feel like the right fit, we can talk about that honestly in your consultation.
