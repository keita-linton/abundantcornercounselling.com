Questions, answered

Straight answers before you book. Anything else — ask in your free consultation.

How do I book a consultation?

Book a free 15-minute consultation through JaneApp, which opens in the same tab. Same-day consultation bookings are available, and Yeukai replies within one business day.

Do I need a referral to book?

No. You do not need a referral from a doctor or anyone else to book a consultation or a session.

How long is a session?

The free consultation is 15 minutes. Individual therapy is 50 minutes, family therapy is 75 minutes, and EMDR is 90 minutes.

How do I pay for a session?

Payment is due at session time, by credit card or e-transfer via Jane. There is no direct billing. You receive a receipt after every paid session for insurance benefits.

What happens if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Please give at least 12 hours' notice. Your first late cancellation or missed appointment is not charged; after that, the full session fee applies.

Are sessions in person or virtual?

All sessions are virtual, by secure video, across Canada, in English and French. You join from a private place, using a device with a camera and microphone.

How much does therapy cost?

The free 15-minute consultation is free. Individual therapy is $120 for 50 minutes, family therapy is $180 for 75 minutes, and EMDR is $260 for 90 minutes. A limited $50-per-session promotion is available for ongoing individual therapy only, while spots last.
