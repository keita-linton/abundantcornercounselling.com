EMDR: healing where it started

Some memories stay stuck, and the body keeps reacting like the hard thing is still happening. EMDR can help people process distressing memories and reduce the emotional distress connected to them. How it unfolds is different for everyone, and we talk through readiness and pacing before any reprocessing begins.

EMDR is one of the approaches offered here for trauma and PTSD, anxiety, depression and grief — by secure video in Edmonton, Saskatoon, Prince Albert and Toronto, and across Canada.

What EMDR is

EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing) is a therapy that can help your brain process painful memories so they may feel less present and less distressing over time. It is often used for trauma and PTSD. Outcomes vary from person to person, and EMDR is not the right fit for everyone, which is part of what the free consultation is for.

EMDR sessions at Abundant Corner Counselling are 90 minutes and $260, held by secure video across Canada with Yeukai Tsikira, RP (Qualifying), CCC, who is trained in EMDR.

Questions, answered

Straight answers before you book. Anything else — ask in your free consultation.

What is EMDR?

EMDR is a well-established, evidence-based approach for trauma and PTSD. It uses guided eye movements or other bilateral stimulation while you hold a difficult memory lightly in mind.

How long is an EMDR session, and what does it cost?

EMDR sessions are 90 minutes and cost $260. Payment is due at session time, by credit card or e-transfer via Jane, and you receive a receipt after every paid session.

Is EMDR right for me?

That is something to explore together in your free consultation and early sessions. EMDR helps many people with trauma and PTSD, but it is not right for everyone or for every moment. We will talk honestly about whether it fits you.

Do I have to tell you everything that happened in EMDR?

No. You choose what to share and how much detail to give. In EMDR, you do not have to retell every detail for the work to help. Safety and your pace come first.

Is EMDR done online?

All sessions, including EMDR, are virtual, by secure video, across Canada. You join from a private place where you feel safe, using a device with a camera and microphone.

Does the $50 promotion apply to EMDR?

No. The $50-per-session promotion applies to ongoing individual therapy only. It does not apply to family therapy or EMDR.

What happens if I need to cancel an EMDR session?

Please give at least 12 hours' notice. Your first late cancellation or missed appointment is not charged; after that, the full session fee applies.

Can I get a receipt for insurance for EMDR?

Yes. Receipts are provided after every paid session. Check your own plan for coverage of Registered Psychotherapist (Qualifying) or Canadian Certified Counsellor services. There is no direct billing.
