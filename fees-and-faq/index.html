How much does therapy cost at Abundant Corner Counselling?

A 15-minute consultation is free. Individual sessions are $120 for 50 minutes, family sessions are $180 for 75 minutes, and EMDR sessions are $260 for 90 minutes. A limited promotion offers $50 per session locked in for ongoing individual therapy only while spots last; family sessions are not included in the promotion and remain $180, and EMDR sessions are not included and remain $260. You receive a receipt after each session for insurance benefits.

What happens in the free consultation?

Fifteen minutes to talk about what is bringing you to therapy, ask your questions, and see if we are a good fit. If we are not, there is no obligation to book.

Are sessions really available across Canada?

Yes. Abundant Corner Counselling is a fully virtual practice. Sessions are held by secure video, and you can join from anywhere in Canada. There is no in-person office or address. Yeukai practises as a Registered Psychotherapist (Qualifying) registered with CRPO in Ontario and as a Canadian Certified Counsellor (CCC) certified with the CCPA, and you will be told which professional bodies she belongs to before you start. If the rules in your home province affect whether she can work with you, she will confirm that with you in the free consultation, before any paid session is booked.

Who is the therapist?

Yeukai Tsikira, MA, Registered Psychotherapist (Qualifying) with CRPO (registration 18718) and Canadian Certified Counsellor (CCC) with CCPA (member 11246478). She holds an MA in Counselling Psychology from Yorkville University and a BA in Behavioural Science from Burman University, founded the practice in December 2024, and is trained in EMDR.

What does ‘Qualifying’ mean?

Registered Psychotherapist (Qualifying) is a registration category with the College of Registered Psychotherapists of Ontario (CRPO). It means Yeukai is fully registered and authorized to practise psychotherapy in Ontario while completing the final steps toward full, independent registration, including clinical supervision and the registration examination. She is accountable to CRPO’s standards of practice and code of ethics in the same way as every other CRPO registrant. Qualifying is its own registration category, not the same as full, independent registration, and she will explain it plainly if you ask.

What is the cancellation policy?

Your appointment time is reserved just for you. If you need to cancel or reschedule, please give at least 12 hours’ notice, so the time can be offered to someone else. Life happens, and we understand that. Your first late cancellation or missed appointment will not be charged. After that, appointments cancelled or rescheduled with less than 12 hours’ notice, and appointments missed without notice, will be charged the full session fee.

How do I pay?

Payment is due at the time of your session, and you can pay by credit card or e-transfer. All payments are processed through Jane, the secure practice platform used for booking and billing. If you pay by card, you enter your details directly into Jane’s payment system. Your card information is encrypted and processed to PCI security standards. It is never stored on this website, and I never see or have access to your full card number. It works the same way as paying securely through your bank or a hospital portal, so you can enter your card details with confidence. After each paid session, you will receive a detailed receipt, which you can submit to your benefits or insurance provider for reimbursement. Direct billing is not offered yet, so you pay at your session and claim it back through your plan.

Who do you work with?

Adults, teens age 12 and up, and families dealing with anxiety, depression, grief, trauma, PTSD and life transitions. The approach focuses on healing at the root cause, not just coping.

What is EMDR, and is it right for me?

EMDR is a therapy that can help your brain process painful memories so they may feel less present and less distressing over time. It is often used for trauma and PTSD. Outcomes vary, and EMDR is not the right fit for everyone. The EMDR page explains it in plain language, including how preparation and readiness work, and we can talk it through in your consultation.

Can I use my benefits?

You get a detailed receipt after each paid session, which most clients submit to their benefits provider. Coverage depends on your plan, so check whether it covers a Registered Psychotherapist or Canadian Certified Counsellor. Direct billing is not offered yet.

How do I book?

Book a free 15-minute consultation directly on JaneApp using any Book button on this site, or use the short form on the Book page if you prefer to share a few details first. You can also email info@abundantcornercounselling.com or call 780-306-1676. I reply within one business day.

Is virtual therapy private and confidential?

Sessions are held by secure video through Jane, the practice platform used for booking and billing. For your own privacy, join from a private space where you will not be overheard, and use headphones if others are nearby. What you share in session is treated confidentially, with the standard legal and safety limits that apply to therapy, which Yeukai explains before you start.

What if I find it hard to open up, or I do not know where to start?

That is very common, and you do not need the right words before you begin. Sessions move at your pace, you are never required to share more than you are ready to share, and you do not have to retell painful experiences in detail before you feel ready. Part of the work is building enough steadiness and trust to begin.

Can a parent join a teen’s consultation or sessions?

Yes. Parents are welcome to join the free consultation to ask questions and talk about what their teen or family is facing. For ongoing work, how parents are involved in a teen’s sessions is agreed together at the start, balancing the teen’s privacy with the family’s goals.

Is this a crisis service?

No. If you are in immediate danger, call 911, and in Canada you can call or text 988 for the Suicide and Crisis Helpline, any time.
