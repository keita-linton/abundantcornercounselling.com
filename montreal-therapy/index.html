Virtual therapy in French and English · Montreal, Quebec · Eastern Time

French-language therapy in Montreal, from home

Montreal is a city of two languages, four seasons, and a pace all its own — and living here can still feel lonely, heavy, or overwhelming. If you are in Montreal and you are dealing with anxiety, depression, grief, burnout, or trauma, Abundant Corner Counselling offers secure virtual therapy in French and English with Yeukai Tsikira, a Registered Psychotherapist (Qualifying) and Canadian Certified Counsellor. You meet from home, on Eastern Time, with a therapist who works on where the pain started, not just how to cope with it.

Why virtual therapy fits Montreal life

Montreal is dense, bilingual, and always moving — and getting across it takes real time.

Whether you are in the Plateau-Mont-Royal, Mile End, Rosemont, Verdun, Notre-Dame-de-Grâce, Westmount, Old Montreal, or Outremont, an in-person appointment can mean a crowded metro ride or a long drive in snow, on top of a full day. Virtual sessions take that trip away. You log on from a quiet room at home, on your lunch break, or after work, and the hour is yours. Montreal runs on Eastern Time, and sessions are booked in your local time.

How sessions work if you are in Quebec

Please read this honestly before you book, so you know where things stand.

Yeukai is registered as a Registered Psychotherapist (Qualifying) with CRPO in Ontario and is a Canadian Certified Counsellor (CCC) nationally. If you are in Quebec, ask in your free consultation about how sessions work for you. She does not claim Quebec licensure or an OPQ permit. The free consultation is the right place to ask your questions and to decide whether working together is right for you.

Questions people in Montreal ask

Can I do therapy in French?

Yes. Yeukai offers sessions in English and French, so you can work in the language that feels most natural to you. You can mention your preference when you book your free consultation.

Are you licensed in Quebec?

Yeukai is registered as a Registered Psychotherapist (Qualifying) with CRPO in Ontario and is a Canadian Certified Counsellor (CCC) nationally. She does not claim Quebec licensure or an OPQ permit. If you are in Quebec, ask in your free consultation about how sessions work for you.

Do you have an office in Montreal?

No. The practice is virtual-only, with no in-person office. Sessions take place by secure video, so you can meet from anywhere in Montreal — or anywhere in Canada — without travelling across the city.
