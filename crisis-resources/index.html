Free crisis help, right now

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 in Canada, or call 911 in an emergency. This practice is not a crisis service.

Abundant Corner Counselling is a virtual therapy practice, not a crisis service. If you or someone near you is in immediate danger, or you feel you might act on thoughts of harming yourself, please use one of the free resources on this page right now — they are available 24 hours a day, and you do not have to face this alone.

If it is an emergency

Call 911.

If someone is in immediate danger, has acted on thoughts of harm, or needs urgent medical help, call 911. Stay with the person if you safely can, and follow the dispatcher's instructions.

Canada-wide help, 24/7

Free, confidential, and available wherever you are in Canada.

988 Suicide Prevention Helpline — call or text 988, 24/7. Use 988 if you are thinking about suicide, if you are worried about someone else, or if you are in emotional distress and need to talk to someone right now.

Kids Help Phone — call 1-800-668-6868, or text CONNECT to 686868. For children, teens, and young adults in Canada. Yeukai previously served as Supervisor, Crisis Services at Kids Help Phone, and knows the care behind this line.

Hope for Wellness Helpline — call 1-855-242-3310, 24/7. For Indigenous peoples across Canada, in English, French, Cree, Ojibway, and Inuktitut. Use this line for emotional support, crisis support, or help finding culturally safe services. You can call for yourself or for someone you care about.

Help by province

Local lines, when a local voice helps.

Alberta: Distress Line Edmonton 780-482-4357; Distress Centre Calgary 403-266-4357; 211 Alberta.

Saskatchewan: Mobile Crisis Saskatoon 306-933-6200; Prince Albert Mobile Crisis 306-764-1011; 211 Saskatchewan.

Ontario: ConnexOntario 1-866-531-2600; 211 Ontario.

British Columbia: BC Crisis Line 310-6789 (no area code); Crisis Centre BC 1-800-784-2433.

Manitoba: Manitoba Suicide Prevention & Support Line 1-888-322-3019; Klinic Crisis Line 204-786-8686.

Nova Scotia: NS Mental Health Crisis Line 1-888-429-8167.

Quebec: Suicide Prevention 1-866-277-3553; 811 Info-Santé.

If your province is not listed here, 988, Kids Help Phone, and Hope for Wellness are available to you Canada-wide, and 211 can connect you with services in your area.

BounceBack: free support in Ontario and BC

A free, CBT-based programme — Ontario and British Columbia only.

BounceBack is a free, CBT-based programme for people experiencing mild to moderate anxiety or depression. BounceBack is available in Ontario and British Columbia only — it is not available in other provinces.

BounceBack is not a crisis service. If you are in crisis, call or text 988, or call 911 in an emergency.

When to use which resource

A simple guide for a hard moment.

  • Immediate danger or a medical emergency: call 911.
  • Thinking about suicide, or worried about someone who is: call or text 988, 24/7.
  • A child, teen, or young adult who needs to talk: Kids Help Phone, 1-800-668-6868 or text CONNECT to 686868.
  • An Indigenous person seeking culturally safe support: Hope for Wellness, 1-855-242-3310.
  • You want help near you: your provincial crisis line above, or 211 in your province.
  • You want structured support for anxiety or low mood, and you live in Ontario or BC: BounceBack.
  • You are not in crisis, but you want ongoing therapy: book a free 15-minute consultation with Abundant Corner Counselling, and read about individual therapy, anxiety therapy, depression therapy, or trauma and PTSD therapy. Therapy here is virtual, across Canada, in English and French — and it is not a crisis service.

If you are supporting someone else, you do not have to have the right words. Staying with them, listening without judgement, and helping them call one of these numbers can make a real difference.

Is Abundant Corner Counselling a crisis service?

No. This practice is a virtual therapy practice, not a crisis service. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 in Canada, or call 911 in an emergency.

What should I do if I am worried about someone else?

Stay with them if you safely can, listen without judgement, and help them call or text 988, or call 911 if there is immediate danger. You can also call 988 yourself for guidance on how to support them.

Do I have to give my name when I call a crisis line?

Most crisis lines, including 988 and Kids Help Phone, do not require you to give your name. You can share as much or as little as you wish. The person on the line is there to listen and to help you stay safe.

What is BounceBack, and where is it available?

BounceBack is a free, CBT-based programme for mild to moderate anxiety or depression. It is available in Ontario and British Columbia only. It is not a crisis service.

Can I still book therapy if I have used a crisis line?

Yes. Many people use a crisis line in a hard moment and then seek ongoing therapy. When you are not in crisis, you can book a free 15-minute consultation to talk about whether therapy here is a good fit.

You do not have to face this alone

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 now, or call 911 in an emergency. When you are ready for ongoing support, a free consultation is here.
