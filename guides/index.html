How these guides are written

Three things you can count on, in every guide here.

First, plain language. Therapy has enough jargon already. When a term matters — like EMDR, or what “Qualifying” means after a therapist’s title — it gets explained in ordinary words, the first time it appears. You should never need a dictionary to understand your own care.

Second, honesty over promises. These guides will not tell you therapy works for everyone, or that one approach fixes everything. Where the honest answer is “it depends,” that is what you will read — along with what it depends on, so you can judge for yourself.

Third, root-cause healing. That is the framework this practice works from: we work on where the pain started, not just how to cope with it. Coping skills matter, and you will learn some. But the deeper work is understanding what shaped the anxiety, the grief, or the pattern that keeps repeating — and healing there, so you can move from survival to thriving.

Are these guides a substitute for therapy?

No. The guides explain things and help you make an informed decision, but reading about therapy is not the same as doing it. If something here resonates, the next step is a conversation, not more reading.

Do I need a referral to book?

No referral is needed. You can book a free 15-minute consultation directly, and decide from there whether to start therapy.

Can I read these guides if I live outside Canada?

Yes — the guides are general information and free for anyone to read. The practice itself offers virtual therapy across Canada only, so if you live elsewhere, the guides can still help you understand your options, but sessions with Yeukai are for people in Canada.

How do I ask a question the guides do not cover?

Bring it to your free consultation, or email info@abundantcornercounselling.com. Questions are welcome — asking one is never an obligation to book.
